# RecallX 🧠

### AI That Learns How You Learn

RecallX is an AI-powered adaptive learning assistant that helps students
understand what they learn, practice through personalized quizzes,
identify learning gaps, and receive a smart revision plan.

---

## 🚀 Problem

Students often study large amounts of content but do not know:

- What concepts they actually understood
- Which topics they are weak in
- What they should revise
- How they should practice effectively

Traditional learning systems provide the same content to every student.

---

## 💡 Solution

RecallX creates a personalized learning loop:

Study → Practice → Analyze → Identify Gaps → Revise → Improve

The system uses AI to analyze study material and generate
personalized learning support.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📚 AI Study Material Analysis
Students can enter their notes or study material and RecallX analyzes
the important concepts.

### 📝 Personalized Quiz
AI automatically generates multiple-choice questions from the
student's learning material.

### 🎯 Learning Gap Detection
RecallX tracks quiz performance and identifies areas where the
student needs more practice.

### 📊 Performance Score
Students receive an instant score after completing the quiz.

### 🔄 Smart Revision Plan
RecallX recommends what the student should review today,
practice tomorrow, and assess later.

### 💡 AI Learning Insight
The system provides a personalized learning recommendation based
on the student's performance.

---

## 🧠 How RecallX Works

1. Student enters study material
2. Gemini AI analyzes the content
3. Important concepts are extracted
4. Personalized quiz questions are generated
5. Student answers the questions
6. RecallX calculates performance
7. Weak areas are identified
8. Personalized revision is recommended
